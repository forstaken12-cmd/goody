1. Who can use Goody

You must be 18 or older to create an account and use Goody. If you are under 18, please do not sign up.

2. What Goody is

Goody lets you finish little tasks from partners (such as playing a game, trying an app, or taking a survey). When a partner pays us for a finished task, we add points to your account. You can then ask to cash those points out.

Points are not money in a bank. They are a balance in this app. A cash-out is a request. It is not complete until it is approved and sent.

3. Your account

You need an email and a password. Keep them private. You are responsible for what happens on your account. Please confirm your email. We may pause cash outs until you do.

One person should have one account. Do not share accounts, use fake emails, or try to trick the app or our partners.

4. Tasks and points

Partners decide if a task was really finished. A task may show as “checking,” then “paid,” or “rejected.” A partner can later reverse a payment (for example after a chargeback). If that happens, we will remove those points, even if they were already paid.

We may change or remove offers at any time. Demo or sample offers are clearly marked and do not use real partner money.

5. Cash outs

The first cash out may have a lower minimum. After that, each method (Bitcoin, PayPal, or a gift card) has its own minimum. We check these on our server.

We may review, delay, or refuse a cash out if we see signs of fraud, a partner reversal, or missing information. Until a payout provider is connected, an admin may approve a request and send it by hand.

You are responsible for giving a correct payout address or email. We are not responsible for payments sent to the details you give us, or for fees charged by PayPal, a gift-card service, or a Bitcoin wallet.
