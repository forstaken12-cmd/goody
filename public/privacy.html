Privacy Policy

This is a template. Have a lawyer review it before you rely on it. It is not legal advice.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Who this is for

This page explains what Goody collects and how it is used. You must be 18 or older to use Goody.

2. What we collect

  • Your email and a hashed password (we do not store your password in plain text).
  • Whether you agreed to the Terms and confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • Your points ledger, task history, and cash-out requests (including the payout address or email you give us).
  • A simple device note (a fingerprint we use to spot many accounts on one device). This is not used to sell ads.
  • Basic technical data such as IP address and browser type, used for security, rate limits, and fraud checks.

If you finish a task, our partners (for example an offerwall or survey company) may also see an anonymous user id we give them, plus data they collect on their own pages. Their privacy rules apply there too.

3. How we use it

We use this information to run your account, award points, handle cash outs, send email such as a confirmation or password reset, keep the service safe, and follow the law.

We do not sell your email or personal information.

4. Who we share it with

  • Offer and survey partners, so they can credit a finished task to the right account (we send a user id, not your password).
  • Payout providers (such as PayPal, a gift-card service, or a Bitcoin Lightning provider), only when you ask to cash out and only with the details needed to send that payment.
  • Hosting and email providers who help us run the app.
  • Anyone we must share with if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account and ledger records while your account is open and for a reasonable time after, because we may need them for payouts, reversals, and records. You can ask us to close your account. Some records may need to be kept if the law says so.

6. Security

We use hashed passwords, signed partner postbacks, and session cookies that JavaScript cannot read. No system is perfect. Please use a unique password.

7. Your choices

You can update your password, confirm or change your email once that is offered, and ask us to close your account. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have more rights to see, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to certain uses. Contact us to ask.

8. Children

Goody is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If we do, we will change the date at the top and, when the change is big, try to tell you in the app.

10. Contact

The operator of this app should add a real contact email here before launch. Until then, use the contact method listed on the live site once it is filled in.
